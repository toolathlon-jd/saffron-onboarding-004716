Starting at Saffron Systems Lab
======

Saffron Systems Lab studies dependable automation for shared research workflows.

Newcomers begin with the two approved starter resources listed below.

- [Compass Workspace Map](/publication/2032-04-07-slo-716-a) — Interactive map. Locate shared project areas and understand their approved purposes. Use Compass before choosing a project area.
- [Lantern Safety Primer](/publication/2032-04-21-slo-716-b) — Quick-start guide. Review safe handling checkpoints before using shared automation benches. Read Lantern before beginning bench work.
